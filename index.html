Common questions

How is the final price decided?

By the size of the move, the distance, access such as stairs or lifts, and whether you need packing. The estimate gives a range and we confirm it after seeing your inventory.

Can you move on weekends?

Yes. We work seven days a week from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Do you move offices after hours?

Yes. Tell us your building rules and we plan the move around them.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes, when you add the packing service. We bring boxes, wrap and tape.
